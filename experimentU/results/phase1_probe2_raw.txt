All load-bearing bindings are supported:

1. **`incoming_request.requested_date` holds the date to validate** — established by an OBSERVED claim (field exists, type str, value_shape YYYY-MM-DD).

2. **`holidays.date` holds holiday dates** — rests on an INFERRED claim (meaning: "dates on which something is observed or closed"), but this is independently supported by OBSERVED evidence: the collection is named "holidays" and the field is named "date" (both from `source_structure`).

3. **`reservations.date` holds the reservation date** — rests on an INFERRED claim (meaning: "the date the booking is for"), but independently supported by OBSERVED evidence: the collection is named "reservations" and the field is named "date" (both from `source_structure`).

The UNKNOWN claims (whether reservations may fall on holidays, what resource is reserved, single-day vs multi-day, tier meaning) are not load-bearing: the job checks holiday status and reservation status independently, does not identify a resource, and treats each date as a single value.
