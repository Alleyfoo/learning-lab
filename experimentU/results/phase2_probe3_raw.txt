All load-bearing bindings are supported:

1. **`incoming_request.requested_date`** holds the date to validate — **OBSERVED** (source structure + field name).
2. **`holidays.date`** holds holiday dates — **OBSERVED** (collection named "holidays", field "date" with `YYYY-MM-DD` values). The INFERRED meaning claim is supplementary; the binding rests on observed structure and the job's own reference to "a holiday."
3. **`reservations.date`** holds the date a booking is for — **CONFIRMED** (human confirmed the INFERRED meaning).

No UNKNOWN claim is load-bearing: the questions about multi-day bookings, reserved resource identity, and whether reservations may fall on holidays are irrelevant to the three checks the job requires.
