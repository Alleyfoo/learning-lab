## What the branch established

> **Authority proceeds when the declared local evidence is sufficient, and stops
> when it is not.**

Y is the half that makes it useful. A (`sku` 3/3, `code` 2/3) chose `sku`; B
(`sku` 2/3, `code` 3/3) chose `code`. A and B are mirrors, so a system following
the field name passes A and fails B — the binding moved with the evidence. C
(both 3/3) blocked and resumed on one human answer. Six A/B probes made **zero**
needless confirmation requests.

Also settled along the way:

- **Input contract is not a source interpretation.** Provenance is needed where
  meaning came from interpreting somebody else's data, not for the node's own
  declared interface (`experimentW/results/RESULT.md`).
- **A human answer supplies content, it does not bless a guess** — promoted
  claims carry the answer, with the prior interpretation in `superseded_meaning`.
