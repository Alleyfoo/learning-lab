not, which reads as an invalidated experiment rather than a checkout artifact.

renormalizing `git rm --cached -r . && git reset --hard`. `verify_prep.py` now
reports **PREP VERIFICATION PASSED (all 9 checks)**, including the W0D self-test
(all 26 codes exercised) and `test_work_definition.py` 14 passed.
