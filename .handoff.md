## Amendment 002 applied (third commit)

Recorded in `workorder_amendment_002.md`, propagated into the report, Experiment 1, the
falsification ledger and README.

- **B1** Detection floor requires **power**, not just confidence. A floor is a Type II
  statement; α alone bounds the wrong error. Contract now carries `test`, `alpha`, `power`,
  `variance_basis`, `baseline_window`.
- **B2** `independence_assumed: true` must be visible — i.i.d. treatment of monthly series
  biases the floor **optimistically**, the unsafe direction. Declared `safety_factor: 1.5`
  until seasonality is modelled.
- **B2.1** *(non-obvious consequence)* The floor is **not a constant**. Variance estimated from
  unanchored history is contaminated by any drift it contains, widening the floor as the anchor
  ages. A model can lose applicability without the source changing at all.
- **B3** T0–T3 tiers replaced by an **evidence vector of orthogonal dimensions**
  (`semantic_meaning`, `aggregate_correctness`, `structural_fit`). Tiers implied an order the
  relation does not have.
- **B3.1** Freshness is a **per-dimension decay**, not a fourth dimension — meaning stales in
  ~24 periods, reconciliation in ~6, structural fit in 1.
- **B5** Three escalation reasons; reason 3 (**evidence expiry**) is a first-class terminal
  state where *nothing looks wrong*.
- **B6** Preregistered outcomes O1–O5, including **O4 "correct undecidability"** — a miss that
  counts as a correct result. Valid only via the git-commit-ordering protocol in B6.2.
- **B6.3** Added a floor **calibration sweep**: the semantic variants test the *floor claim*,
  not the detector. Miscalibration in either direction is an informative failure of the claim.

**Trap for whoever runs the experiment:** the detection floor must be committed to git *before*
the drift corpus exists. If that ordering is broken, O3 and O4 are unfalsifiable and the run
must be repeated. Do not tune the floor after seeing results.
